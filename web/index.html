Doppelhood

Pick a neighborhood you know and see what feels like it in another city.

Scores compare each pair with every neighborhood's best match in another city. A score of 50 means the pair is as close as a typical best match, and a low score means even the closest neighborhood in that city is a stretch. Each neighborhood is compared on its Wikipedia article (character), the Wikivoyage listings located in it (food, nightlife, sights, activities, shopping), and the mix of restaurants, bars, shops, and parks mapped in OpenStreetMap within 500 meters (street life). Every signal is measured against the rest of its own city first, so a match means two neighborhoods play the same role in their cities.

Summaries and text-based scores come from Wikipedia and Wikivoyage under CC BY-SA 4.0; each neighborhood name links to its article and author history. Street-life scores use map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the ODbL. Neighborhood lists and locations come from Wikidata (CC0). Source on GitHub.
