Doppelhood

Pick a San Francisco neighborhood and see its closest equivalents in another city.

Scores are percentiles. A score of 90 means the pairing is more similar than 90% of all pairings between a San Francisco district and a district in another city. Similarity is measured section by section on each district's Wikivoyage article, after removing what all districts in the same city have in common, so a match means two districts play the same role in their own cities.

District text and summaries are from Wikivoyage, used under CC BY-SA 4.0. Each district name links to its article and author history. Scores are derived from that text and shared under the same license. Source on GitHub.
